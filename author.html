WHY I CREATED THE EVERLIGHT CHRONICLES

Stories can light the way.

Through The Everlight Chronicles, Robert Watson III creates epic, faith-forward fantasy stories that help children recognize truth, resist lies, and grow in their relationship with Christ — not through preaching, but through unforgettable adventure, real characters, and timeless lessons.

The heart of the series is simple: great storytelling can entertain while also helping a brighter generation grow in truth, courage, hope, compassion, humility, and purpose.
