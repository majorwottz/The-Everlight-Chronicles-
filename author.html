FROM THE AUTHOR

Why I created Everlight.

I wanted to create the kind of adventure a child could get completely lost in — a world filled with danger, wonder, friendship, courage and mystery — while quietly carrying truths that matter long after the final page.

At the heart of this series are four children. They are not chosen because they are perfect. They are chosen because they are willing. Their journey is about learning to recognize truth, resist lies, stand when they are afraid, hold onto hope, and show compassion when it costs something.

My hope is that young readers see something of themselves in Kai, Zemi, Finn and Lila — and discover that courage, truth, hope and compassion are not just powers in a fantasy world. They are choices we can make every day.

A NOTE TO READERS & FAMILIES

Welcome to the Everlight.

Thank you for stepping into this world with me. The Everlight Chronicles was built to be experienced together — read, discussed, imagined, and remembered. I hope these stories create conversations between children and the people who love them, and I hope every reader leaves the adventure carrying a little more courage, truth, hope and compassion into their own world.
