THE STORY BEHIND THE STORY

Why I Created The Everlight Chronicles

Robert Watson III created The Everlight Chronicles to give young readers an unforgettable fantasy adventure grounded in truth, courage, hope, compassion, humility, and redemption.

The story follows four ordinary children who are chosen not because they are perfect, but because they are willing to carry the Light when fear, grief, doubt, and deception close in.

“My mission is to help children recognize truth, resist lies, and grow in their relationship with Christ through story—without preaching at the reader.”
